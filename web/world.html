Open globe ↗

On the Alice computer, open PowerShell in your Alice project and run:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/setup-gods-eye.ps1

Then restart Alice and return here.

The globe opens in a separate tab and runs only on this computer. Stop it here to release its resources. Alice also stops the process it launched when Alice shuts down.

01 / EXPLORE

Start without keys

Use the included basemap and public feeds. Live-feed availability depends on each source and your connection. Some overlays are simulations or estimates.

02 / POWER UP

Add optional providers

Use POWER UP inside the globe for photorealistic maps, additional feeds, or its built-in voice assistant. Provider keys, terms, and quotas apply. Alice’s credentials are not copied over.

03 / ASK ALICE

“Open World View”

Use Alice’s voice navigation to reach this page. Globe camera control and scene questions use God’s Eye View’s own microphone and optional OpenAI configuration.
